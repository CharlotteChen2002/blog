This is a summary of my Fall 2024 semester at Columbia University. I will share my experience and thoughts about the courses I took, the events I attended, and the progress I made. I will also discuss my goals for the upcoming semester and reflect on what I learned this semester.

## Courses
At the beginning of the semester, I planned to take the following courses:
- **ELEN E3201: Circuit Analysis**
- **ELEN E3801: Signals and Systems**
- **CSEE W4868: System-On-Chip Platforms**
- **CSEE W4824: Computer Architecture**
- **EECS E4321: Digital VLSI Circuits**
- **JPNS G4007: Intro to Classical Japanese**  

While circuit analysis and signals were mandatory for my major, other courses were electives that I chose based on my interests. However, I underestimated the workload of these courses and my schedule soom became a misery. I had to drop the digital VLSI, which I knew nothing before, before the first exam. Then, after got 40/100 in the midterm of the intro to classical Japanese, dropping the couse was the only possiblity in my _Decision FSM_ (No such thing in the world).  

The only good thing left was that I got good grades in rest of the courses. Good ending, right?
